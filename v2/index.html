02 · Colour

Deep black and IPX Orange, lit like a stage. Nothing else.

The gradient#EF4136 → #FBB040Logo X · streaks only
  • Every surface starts black. Atmosphere comes from orange light laid over black, never from a lighter base.
  • The gradient lives in two places: the logo X and the streak texture. Never a button, never a text fill, never a wash. Stops come from the logo master, not from a colour table.
  • Text on orange is black. White on IPX Orange fails contrast at any size.
  • No second accent. No blue, purple, green or grey palettes.
03 · Type

Geist for everything. Three cuts, three jobs.

The headline is the picture. Everything else on the frame is small.

Geist Black · headlines
Fans make culture.

Huge, tight, 2–5 words, two lines max. Sentence case with a full stop. Caps only on the banner line.

Geist Medium · everything readable
For the fans. For the culture.

Payoffs, body, CTAs, URLs. Never bold a payoff; its contrast with the Black headline is the system.

Geist Mono · small labels
Creators. Brands. Communities.

Verb chip, small-caps footers, legal, DEMO tags. Uppercase, tracked wide.

  • Don't substitute Inter, Roboto or any other face. Don't mix weights inside a headline. Don't set body in Mono. No serifs anywhere.

05 · Voice

Very simple. Straightforward. Premium.

Short sentences. One idea per post. Confident, never loud. Lead with what IPX does, then the proof.

Do

  • Write like a premium brand: short, calm, specific
  • Lead with what IPX does, then the proof
  • One clear CTA: ipx.exchange
  • 1–2 hashtags max, only when useful
  • Same look on every post: black, orange, Geist
  • Reply daily, in this voice, to mentions and key accounts

Don't

  • Hype: "LFG", "let's goooo", rocket strings, ALL CAPS
  • Price talk, "earn", "rewards", "trading", "invest"
  • Funding or valuation numbers, including reposts that quote them
  • Hashtag walls or one-word posts
  • Mixed names for the same thing (Committee / Board / Council)

08 · Before anything posts

Nine checks. A frame that fails one does not post.

  • Black ground, one orange light, Geist. Nothing else.
  • Headline 2–5 words, two lines max.
  • One CTA: ipx.exchange.
  • Text on orange is black, not white.
  • No earn / rewards / price / invest / trading / funding / valuation wording.
  • No LFG, rockets, hashtag walls or ALL CAPS (banner line excepted).
  • One name per thing. Campaign: FAN SZN. Points: Aura.
  • Legal on the image where a post touches token, competition or prizes.
  • IPX written approval on any token or reward wording.
